step 1: document upload & text extraction

you upload your resume in any format — pdf, docx, txt, or image files (png, jpg, jpeg). our document ingestion pipeline handles multi-column layouts, tables, and unconventional styling with tesseract ocr fallback for scanned documents.

pdf parsing docx extraction ocr image processing layout noise removal
2

step 2: deep semantic resume understanding

our nlp engine parses your resume into structured sections: contact information, education, career experience, personal projects, and certifications. technical skills are normalized using our 178+ skill taxonomy (e.g. converting "js" to "javascript", "k8s" to "kubernetes", or "ml" to "machine learning").

personal information education history work experience 178+ skill taxonomy synonym normalization
3

step 3: job requirement analysis

choose an active role from our platform database or paste any external job posting from linkedin or indeed. jobalign ai extracts the core role requirements, separates must-have required skills from preferred skills, and establishes experience benchmarks.

required skills isolation preferred skills detection experience duration target education level target
4

step 4: multi-signal compatibility scoring

rather than a naive keyword count, jobalign ai computes an objective weighted score across 5 essential dimensions of job fit:

skills match (40%)
overlap with core technical requirements
experience fit (20%)
years of experience and seniority level
semantic fit (20%)
cosine similarity of dense embeddings
education fit (10%)
degree level and field alignment
project relevance (10%)
project technical stack relevance
5

step 5: explainable results & actionable recommendations

you receive a complete breakdown: matched skills highlighted in green, missing skills identified in amber, partial matches with reasoning, and specific recommendations on what skills or projects to add to maximize your chances.

✓ matched skills ○ missing skills partial match reasoning ats improvement advice
